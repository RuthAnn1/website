About me

I build websites that are thoughtful, responsive, and easy to read.

I love meeting new people, sharing ideas, and helping others learn. My background in education and technology gives me a strong foundation for building meaningful digital experiences.

See projects

When I was young, I often walked up to strangers and introduced myself. I believe everyone has a story, and that curiosity shaped my path into web design and development.

I moved from education into computer information systems, where I learned how to turn ideas into working projects. One of my favorite assignments was a Python game called My Pet, where players cared for a virtual creature and I worked with a teammate and an artist to bring it to life.

What I enjoy

Designing clear layouts, crafting helpful content, and turning complex ideas into easy-to-use pages. I enjoy presenting projects, teaching others, and working on websites that look polished on every device.

Career highlights

Education & communication

Project presentations
