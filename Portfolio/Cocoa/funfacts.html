Fun Facts About Cocoa

Here is some fun information about the cocoa bean

  • Cocoa tree is only found in the tropical rainforests

  • It takes 5 years for cocoa trees to produce cocoa beans

  • We spell cocoa this way because of a spelling mistake made in the 18th century

  • The fruit of the cocoa tree are the cocoa pods themselves

  • 400 cocoa beans are needed to make one pound of chocolate

  • The cocoa beans were eaten by the native tribes as early as 600AD

  • Even though Europe was importing the cocoa bean at 1585 the first chocolate bar was not invented till the late 1800s

  • The cocoa bean has caffeine in it

  • The cocoa bean has been used for currency

  • Having cocoa beans was a sign of wealth

  • Cocoa plants cannot be in direct sunlight otherwise they will die

  • December 13th is national cocoa day

  • The cocoa bean has health benefits
