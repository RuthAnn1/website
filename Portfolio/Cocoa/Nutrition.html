Strong Immunity

Cocoa is full of nutrients that help strengthen people’s immunity. Some of the minerals that are in cocoa are , zinc and selenium. All three of these minerals are needed by your body to have strong immunity. Also because of the zinc located in cocoa it can help heal wounds. Especially the foods that are 90% cocoa. The stronger the cocoa the more zinc the food will have. Zinc is a mineral that will help injuries heal faster.

May Help With Depression

Cocoa has phenethylamine. This substance helps as a natural neurostimulator and can be used as a antidepressant.

Inflammation

Inflammation is something many people struggle with. However, cocoa is rich in theobromine. This alkaloid is known to help reduce inflammation. Because of the reduction of inflammation, it also helps people stay protected from a variety of diseases. Which include but are not limited to heat disease, diabetes, and cancer.

Cholesterol

Cocoa has been studied and seen to help people lower their LDL(bad cholesterol). It also increases blood flow which reduces blood clots and also helps to lower blood pressure in some people.
