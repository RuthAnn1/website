The Husky

History: Huskies are a new breed to America than many people originally think. They were brought over here in 1908. They are indeed a very new breed comparatively to the other dog breeds that we have come to know and love. This breed started out in the country of Russia. They were brought over to the US for sled dog races. However, because of their small size many people thought they would not be able to complete the races. The huskies that were brought to the United States indeed did not win the sled dog race. However, they did come in third which gave them a name here for being sled dogs.

Temperament:As seen in their history these dogs were bread for running sled dog races. Therefore, these are high energy dogs. They need to be waked/ran every day. They are also intelligent dogs that will need a job to do. They need an owner that is ready to keep up with their high energy and intelligence. This breed also likes to use their voice. Even though they do not bark they love to howl. If you are not a person that is ok with noise this breed is not for you.

The Greenland Dog

History:The Greenland dog is a very ancient wolflike breed. These dogs were also around over 4,000 years ago. However, unlike the malamute historical records show that this breed was often eaten by the Inuit people. Through a variety of studies, it is believed that genetics of the Greenland dog is closer to the 9,000 year remains found of an ancient dog than any of the other Alaskan dogs that we have today. Which makes many people think that this specific breed is quite ancient comparatively.

Temperament:This dog is not one of the smartest breeds compared to the other sled dog breeds. They can be difficult to train. This tracks with their history. The Greenland dog was not originally bred to be a household pet, nor were they bred to be a working dog to help the humans with a task. Therefore, the breed is not the most intelligent. Even though this dog was not bred to be a companion it is an extremely friendly dog that loves all people and yes that means strangers. They would not be a good guard dog for that reason. The Malamute

History: The Malamute originally was bred to pull heavy sleds in the dense snow of Alaska. They are one of the largest of the Alaskan dogs. These dogs can weigh up to 80lbs WOW! Malamutes were also used to hunt seals and polar bears in their distant past. These dogs are believed to have settled in Alaska more than 4,000 years ago. They have been here much longer than many of the Alaskan dogs.

Temperament: Because of their large size this breed would not work well in apartment living. Because they were originally bred to hunt polar bears and seals this breed has a very high prey drive. This means that Malamutes would not work well in a house with smaller animals. They are friendly dogs and like to be around their people however can be reserved with strangers. The Samoyed

History:This breeds history is similer to the Malamute. However, instead of hunting seals and polar bears the Samoyed was trained to help hunt/herd reindeer. They were also used to hall sleds fir the Samoyed people. These dogs differ from their counterparts because they were originally bred to be a family dog. Most of the other dogs were bred for food or just working. Companionship was not on the minds of the ancient people that owned the dogs. Click here to view a youtube video on the breed.

Temperament:This dog has a loving personality and is highly intelligent. However, because of their history they can be very independent as well. They were bread as family pets centuries ago, so they are a breed that thrives on being around their people and family. This makes them not good guard dogs.

Alaskan Klee Kai

History:The Klee Kai was bred by miniaturizing the husky breed. This breed is fairly new to the Alasken dog breeds. They only started to get bred in the 1970’s by Linda Spurlin when she fell in love with a small husky. Staring with her new companion she started breeding down the Alaskan huskies to create her new breed of dog.

Temperament:This dog is a high activity dog. Which means just 1 short walk a day will not satisfy it. Klee Kai need to have a running buddy and have a lot to do. These dogs are also highly intelligent and need an owner that knows what they are doing. The Klee Kai is not a first dog owner breed. They need someone that understands dogs very well.
