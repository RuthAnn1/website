Fur

Because sled dogs have been bred for centuries for a large variety of tasks such as: herding reindeer, pulling sleds, and hunting, they require specific care according to their breed. These dogs have extremely thick dense fur. They have needed the thick fur to stay warm in northern Russia and Alaska. They will need to be brushed frequently to keep their fur looking pristine. Sled dogs also will need to stay cool. They are built for the northern climates so if they live in the warm south there are some things to keep in mind for your furry companion. First make sure during the hottest part of the day keep the dog indoors with the Air Conditioning on. Taking them outside to play and run off energy should be done in the cooler parts of the day such as early in the morning or at dusk.

Energy

All of these dogs are high energy dogs. This means that they need to be worked physically every day. They are not types of dogs that are happy just sitting and reading a book. They need to be walked, run, and/or biked everyday to be at their happiest.

Intelegence

Alaskan dogs are extremely smart and will need their brains exercised daily. They will learn new task quickly; however, they are also independent and head strong dogs. They will keep testing you to make sure you are the pack leader. You will need to know how to take control of situations and make sure your new pet knows that you are the pack leader.
