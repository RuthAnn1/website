Events!

Pet Events

Welcome to the events page! Here you can find the different pet events in the area. Along with the events that can be found in Bismarck the dates marked with the green tabs are events I am running for the community. These events include but are not limited to; introduction to puppy classes, interviews with breeders in the area to educate on the different breeds, sport training and more. If you have any questions feel free to contact me by clicking the button below.

March 15th

Puppy Class Orientation

Lewis and Clark Veterinary Clinc

This orientation class will be attended without your puppy so you focus on learning yourself. The subsequent 5 classes will be attended with your puppy. During orientation we focus on discussing how puppies learn, expectations for class and trouble shooting any currently occurring problems you are having. We will cover training sit, come, lay down, leave it, and the beginning of leash skills. During subsequent classes puppies will get a little bit of social time at the end of class to play with their classmates.
